I created a piece that uses the modal synthesis library to add color to an underwater atmosphere. 

https://amarantheum.github.io/assets/audio/modal_example.mp3

The water/bubble sounds are synthesized in MaxMSP using randomized sine waves that pitch up. Reverb was used to make the drips fuller and more atmospheric.

A tonal sample of piano was used to create the resonant spaces that add the color to the atmosphere as the piece progresses.
