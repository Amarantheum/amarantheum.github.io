Created an application for drawing patterns from a harmonograph generated by two sphereical pendulums—one with a pen and one with a page. 
* Derived the equations of motion for spherical pendulums using Langrangian Mechanics
* Applied the Runge-Kutta numerical approximation method to solve differential equations
* Calculated the correct intersection between the pen and the paper using linear algebra
