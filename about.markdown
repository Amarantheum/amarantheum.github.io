Justin Bruss is a recent graduate from the University of British Columbia and currently lives in Portland, Oregon. He was the head of mechanical design for team 8949 which was the Oregon state champion for years 2017 and 2018. He has been playing piano for 14 years and been a music producer for 8 years. He is excited to combine his interest in music with his interest in both computing and mechanical design. His favorite programming language is Rust.
